## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury dark aesthetic tailored for elite bespoke tattoo ateliers, master artists, and discerning clientele. The platform bridges editorial fine-art exhibition with clinical operational precision.

### Aesthetics & Tone
- **Atmospheric & Tactile Minimalism**: Dominated by deep charcoal voids, delicate champagne-gold line work, and deep chiaroscuro contrasts.
- **Editorial Exhibitionism**: Portfolio imagery takes precedence as gallery-grade fine art, flanked by understated metadata.
- **Surgical Precision**: Operational interfaces (scheduling, deposits, release forms) exude confidence, legibility, and architectural rigor.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined, low-glare workspace that mirrors the ambiance of a private, high-end studio suite.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas**: `#0D0D11` — Deep obsidian charcoal, absorbing ambient light.
- **Surface Level 1**: `#15161E` — Primary panels, sidebar trays, and sheet drawers.
- **Surface Level 2**: `#1E1F2B` — Cards, floating navigation headers, and segmented controls.
- **Surface Level 3**: `#272838` — Hover fills, modal surfaces, and interactive card states.
- **Ghost Borders**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` — Precise hairline division across all dark surfaces.
- **Accent Hairlines**: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` — Used exclusively for focused states and hero visual framing.

### Brand Accents
- **Primary Gilt Accent**: `#D4AF37` — Metallic gold applied to primary CTAs, active status highlights, and featured badges.
- **Secondary Champagne**: `#E6C875` — Shimmer highlights, key metrics, and hover transitions on gold elements.

### Operational Status Palette
- **Pending**: `#F59E0B` (Amber) on `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` fill with `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)` border.
- **Confirmed**: `#10B981` (Emerald Green) on `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` fill with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)` border.
- **Cancelled**: `#EF4444` (Crimson Red) on `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)` fill with `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)` border.
- **Finished**: `#94A3B8` (Slate Grey) on `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)` fill with `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.25)` border.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between avant-garde artistic authority and structural data clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (`Syne`)**: Expressive, sculptural, and unapologetically bold. Used for portfolio headers, artist monographs, and high-level studio metrics.
- **Body & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean geometric grotesque with high legibility. Used for scheduling matrices, deposit figures, client medical notes, and session durations.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: High tracking (`0.12em`) combined with uppercase formatting (`label-caps`) delivers luxury brand poise for studio statuses, dates, and category tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly disciplined 12-column asymmetric fluid grid orchestrates both operational dashboards and freeform editorial galleries.

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: Single-column stack with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Sticky bottom-dock operational controls.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1199px`)**: 6-column fluid structure. Navigation collapses to an icon bar; calendar views condense into single-day feeds.
- **Desktop (`1200px+`)**: 12-column layout with fixed 260px utility sidebar and fluid canvas. `margin: 3rem` frames the operational desk, preserving substantial negative space around portfolio exhibitions.

### Rhythmic Alignments
- Editorial media layouts alternate between wide panoramic spans (12 columns) and offset detail studies (4–6 columns).
- Scheduling grids strictly anchor to consistent `space-md` (1rem) row strides for half-hour appointment slots.

## Elevation & Depth

This system shuns muddy generic drop shadows, deriving visual depth entirely from light discipline, layered luminance tiers, and surgical perimeter hairlines.

### Tiered Surface Stacking
- **Canvas (`#0D0D11`)**: Level 0 foundation.
- **Panels & Columns (`#15161E`)**: Level 1 structural tier, defined by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Cards & Modals (`#1E1F2B`)**: Level 2 focal elements, defined by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Elevated Overlays (`#272838`)**: Level 3 flyouts and context menus, paired with an ambient tinted shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.

### Gold Illumination
Focused cards, ongoing live sessions, and active booking slots feature an ambient gold aura:
`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4);`

## Shapes

The interface employs restrained geometric softness (`roundedness: 1`), conveying bespoke craft, architectural solidity, and mechanical poise.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Checkboxes, status indicators, and micro tags.
- **Card & Input Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Form inputs, calendar blocks, client detail panels, and photo gallery cells.
- **Modal & Container Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px`)**: Booking drawers, consent sign-off sheets, and artwork inspection modals.
- **Circular (`9999px`)**: Artist avatars, circular tool tips, and floating playhead controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#D4AF37` fill with `#0D0D11` label in `Plus Jakarta Sans` SemiBold. Hover scales background to `#E6C875` with subtle gold bloom.
- **Secondary (Outlined)**: Transparent background, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and border to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost Action**: Text `#E6C875` or muted `#94A3B8`, no background or border. Underlined on focus.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Compact capsules adhering to `label-caps`. 
- Padding: `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- Rendered with 10–12% tinted alpha backgrounds, matching 30% border stroke, and bright status-colored text (`#F59E0B` Pending, `#10B981` Confirmed, `#EF4444` Cancelled, `#94A3B8` Finished).
- Include an optional 6px circular glowing indicator dot.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark container fill `#15161E` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Active focus state immediately replaces border with 1px `#D4AF37` and adds a subtle gold aura.
- Labels sit above the field using `label-caps` in `#94A3B8`.

### Appointment & Portfolio Cards
- Background: `#1E1F2B` framed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Portfolio Media Card**: Frameless photography container with an aspect ratio of 4:5 or 1:1. On hover, overlay transitions to a rich 60% gradient reveal featuring the artist name, style tags, and session duration.
- **Booking Session Tile**: Visual time-block on the daily studio schedule. Left-hand edge marked by a 3px status bar (Amber/Green/Crimson/Slate). Displays client name, piece placement, and total quoted deposit.

### Interactive Tattoo Anatomy Map
- Stylized, minimal charcoal wireframe SVG body model.
- Selected placement areas highlight in `#D4AF37` with animated radial pulse rings indicating current WIP projects.
